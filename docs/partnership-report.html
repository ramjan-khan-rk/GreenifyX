Summary

What to say in the first minute.

The problem. Most sustainability apps raise awareness but can't show that anything actually happened. Partners can't tell a real tree planting from a stock photo, and residents lose interest when nothing they do is recognised.

What GreenifyX does. It makes environmental action visible, verified and rewarding. A resident posts an action such as planting a Ghaf, recycling or a beach clean-up. Friends, an AI check, GPS geofences and field verifiers confirm it. Only verified actions earn Eco Points, which people redeem for partner rewards, donate to real tree planting, or use to climb neighbourhood and emirate leaderboards.

What partners get. A web dashboard with live participation, estimated CO₂ impact by action and by emirate, campaign tools with GPS-verified attendance, citizen issue reports on a map, recycling pickups, events, and ESG reports for organisations.

Where it stands. The platform is built and running as a UAE pilot. A full technical check on 14 November 2026 found no broken connections between the apps and the server, and all 145 automated tests pass. Ten features added this week still need testing on real phones, and a few items listed below should be finished before onboarding a government partner.

CO₂ figures in GreenifyX are indicative per-action estimates, not certified measurement (MRV). Say this plainly to government audiences; it builds trust. The impact passport proposed in “Next 5 features” is the path to auditable figures.

How it works

One loop, from a resident's action to a partner's report.

1 · ActResident does something real and posts it with a photo or video.
2 · VerifyA friend confirms, AI checks the photo, or GPS and a field verifier confirm attendance.
3 · RewardVerified actions earn Eco Points, badges and streaks.
4 · RedeemPoints buy partner rewards or fund a real tree in My Forest.
5 · ReportPartners see participation, CO₂ estimates and emirate breakdowns.

Why the numbers can be trusted

  • Points only after verification. Posting alone earns nothing until a friend, the AI check or an admin verifies it.
  • Anti-cheat. The same photo can't be posted twice, AI flags off-topic or reused images, and campaign reports detect fake GPS.
  • Geofenced campaigns. Participation counts only inside the campaign area and time window, with field verifiers approving reports.
  • Points ledger. Every point has an auditable record that can be reversed, and nothing is changed silently.
  • Moderation. Members can report posts, and admins can hide content and suspend accounts with a stated reason.

What needs fixing

Things that are wrong today. None of them stops a demo.

Fixed on 14 November 2026: deleting an account now also removes that person's utility bills and bill photos, synced steps, Eco Academy progress, household membership and open brand deal applications. Funded trees and paid deal records stay, without personal details.
Bill scans are lost if the server restarts

A scanned bill's reading is held in server memory for 30 minutes before saving. If Render restarts in that window, the member has to scan again. Move it to the database.

README is out of date

The project README doesn't list this week's ten features or the new settings. Partners and developers often read it first.

Missing small screens

Members can't delete a typed-in bill or see a list of their QR check-ins, even though the server supports both.

Some Arabic reads unevenly

A few sentences built from parts, such as the Wrapped banner, may read awkwardly right to left. Some server messages, like “Needs 100+ followers”, are English only.

Built, but not finished

These work, but have a gap to close or a setting to switch on.

Not yet tested on real phones

  • Green Wrapped, Health sync, My bills, Do it too, My Forest, auto-translate, Eco Lens, Household, Eco Academy and Brand deals were built and pass automated checks, but nobody has used them on a phone yet.
  • Health sync needs the HealthKit capability switched on in Xcode for iPhone (steps are in the iOS build guide). On Android it raised the minimum to Android 8.0.

Known limits

  • Calls ring only when the friend's app is open. If it's closed, the caller sees “isn't online” and the friend gets a missed-call notification. Ringing a locked phone needs CallKit (iPhone) and ConnectionService (Android).
  • Brand deal payouts are manual. Admins pay creators by bank transfer and mark them paid. There's no automatic payout yet.
  • No admin pages yet for Health sync, utility bills, households or Eco Academy. Lessons are fixed in the code and can't be edited from the dashboard.
  • Eco Lens is drawn effects, not face-tracking camera filters like Instagram's.
  • Reports aren't downloadable files. ESG and impact reports are web pages, and analytics export is “copy CSV” only. Government partners will usually ask for PDF and Excel.
  • No UAE PASS sign-in. It's on the planned list.
  • CO₂ is estimated, not certified, and challenge progress is self-reported.
  • One server instance. Reminders run inside the single Render server, and on a free plan the server sleeps and the app shows a “waking up” message.
  • Testing depth. The admin has no automated tests and there are no end-to-end tests across app, server and admin.

Next 5 features

Chosen to win and keep partnerships with UAE authorities and communities. Each has a mobile side and an admin side. Effort is a rough guide for one developer.

1. UAE PASS sign-in and verified residents

Effort: medium

Government partners need to know participants are real UAE residents. UAE PASS is the national digital identity, so it removes fake accounts and makes reports credible.

Mobile

“Sign in with UAE PASS”, a verified-resident badge, and faster sign-up with no email code.

Admin

Filter and report by verified residents only, with verified-resident share shown on every dashboard and export.

2. Partner portal with downloadable reports

Effort: medium

Each authority or company wants its own view and a monthly report it can forward, not access to everyone's data.

Mobile

Partner-branded campaign and challenge pages, so residents see who runs them.

Admin

Partner logins that only see their own campaigns, areas and members. Monthly PDF and Excel impact reports, sent by email automatically.

3. Recycling drop-off network

Effort: medium

Municipalities measure recycling in kilograms at collection points. Linking app activity to real drop-offs gives them figures they already report on.

Mobile

Scan the QR code on a recycling station or reverse-vending machine to log a drop-off and earn points. Find the nearest station with live status.

Admin

Manage stations, see drop-offs and estimated kilograms per station and area, and flag stations that are full or misused.

4. Neighbourhood leagues and targeted missions

Effort: small to medium

Authorities want action where it's needed, such as one beach, one district or one week. Local pride drives participation.

Mobile

Leaderboards by neighbourhood and emirate, plus missions that appear only for people in a chosen area, with a push alert when one starts nearby.

Admin

Draw an area on the map, launch a mission to people there, and watch results on the heat map in real time.

5. Impact passport and audit trail

Effort: medium to large

Turns estimates into evidence. Schools and employers can recognise verified volunteer hours, and authorities can audit any number on a report.

Mobile

A personal impact passport with verified volunteer hours and actions, and a QR code a school or employer can scan to confirm them.

Admin

A trail from every reported figure back to the actions and verifiers behind it, a third-party auditor role, random-sample checks, and evidence exports.

Also worth doing soon, though not headline features: calls that ring when the app is closed, admin pages for bills, households and Eco Academy, and automatic payouts for brand deals.

Presentation outline

A suggested 10-slide deck for a partnership meeting, built from this report.

  1. Title. GreenifyX: verified environmental action for the UAE. Your name and the pilot status.
  2. The problem. Awareness doesn't show impact, and partners can't verify participation.
  3. The loop. Act, verify, reward, redeem, report (use the five steps above).
  4. Live demo. Post an action, verify it, see points, open the admin overview. Use demo data.
  5. Trust. Verification layers, anti-cheat, the points ledger, and honest CO₂ estimates.
  6. What you get. The panel for the partner in the room, from “What each partner gets”.
  7. Built for the UAE. All seven emirates, Arabic and English, Ghaf and mangrove planting, DEWA, ADDC and SEWA bills, Asia/Dubai time.
  8. Pilot proposal. One campaign or one district for 8–12 weeks, with agreed measures such as participants, verified actions and kilograms collected.
  9. Roadmap. The next 5 features, with UAE PASS and the partner portal first.
  10. The ask. A named contact, a pilot area, data and branding permissions, and a review date.

Before a demo

Do these so nothing fails in front of a partner.

  • Wake the server a few minutes early, or move Render to a paid plan so it doesn't sleep.
  • Load demo data with npm run seed:demo: 24 members across all seven emirates, 96 posts, challenges and comments. Remove it afterwards with npm run seed:demo -- --clear; real accounts are never touched.
  • Check the server settings above are set, especially the AI key, Firebase and the TURN relay.
  • Install the latest build on two phones, and try each new feature once in English and once in Arabic.
  • Change the TURN relay password, since it was shared in chat.
