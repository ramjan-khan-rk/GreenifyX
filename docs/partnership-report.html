Summary

What to say in the first minute.

The problem. Most sustainability apps raise awareness but can't show that anything actually happened. Partners can't tell a real tree planting from a stock photo, and residents lose interest when nothing they do is recognised.

What GreenifyX does. It makes environmental action visible, verified and rewarding. A resident posts an action such as planting a Ghaf, recycling at a station or a beach clean-up. Friends, an AI check, GPS geofences, station QR codes and field verifiers confirm it. Only verified actions earn Eco Points, which people redeem for partner rewards, donate to real tree planting, or use to climb neighbourhood and emirate leagues.

What partners get. Their own partner portal with live campaigns, members and impact, monthly PDF and Excel reports, targeted missions for a neighbourhood, recycling station volumes, and an audit trail that traces every number back to the actions and verifiers behind it. Residents can sign in with UAE PASS, so authorities can count verified residents.

Where it stands. The platform is built and running as a UAE pilot. A full check on 14 November 2026 found all 280 app and dashboard calls connected to the server, and all 176 automated tests pass. Several features added this week still need testing on real phones before launch.

CO₂ figures are indicative per-action estimates, not certified measurement (MRV). Say this plainly to government audiences. The impact passport and audit trail show exactly how each figure was verified, which is what builds trust.

How it works

One loop, from a resident's action to a partner's report.

1 · ActResident does something real and posts it, or scans a recycling station.
2 · VerifyA friend, the AI check, GPS, a station QR or a field verifier confirms it.
3 · RewardVerified actions earn Eco Points, mission bonuses and league standing.
4 · RedeemPoints buy partner rewards or fund a real tree in My Forest.
5 · ReportPartners see live numbers, download reports and audit any figure.

Why the numbers can be trusted

  • Points only after verification. Posting alone earns nothing until it's verified.
  • Anti-cheat. The same photo can't be posted twice, AI flags off-topic images, campaign reports detect fake GPS, and station drop-offs only earn points at the station.
  • UAE PASS. Residents who sign in with the national digital identity are marked as verified residents. Their Emirates ID number is never stored.
  • Points ledger and audit trail. Every point has a record. Auditors can sample actions at random, recheck them, and reverse points that fail.
  • Moderation. Members report posts, and admins hide content and suspend accounts with a stated reason.

Test the new features on real phones

Especially ringing a locked phone, Health sync, scanning a bill, scanning a station and UAE PASS sign-in. Build the Android app once to confirm the new native call code compiles.

Switch services on

UAE PASS needs onboarding with TDRA to get its keys. iPhone ringing needs an Apple push key. Brand deal payouts need Stripe Connect turned on. HealthKit must be ticked in Xcode.

Known limits

Eco Lens effects are drawn on the photo, not face-tracking. Video pauses when the app goes to the background on iPhone (an Apple rule), though voice continues. PDF reports show non-Latin names as "?"; the Excel version keeps them. A few server messages are still English only.

Next 5 features

The five proposed last time are now built. These are the next five, chosen for authorised communities and government partners.

1. Official community pages

Effort: medium

Verified organisations, such as municipal departments, NGOs, schools and resident associations, get their own page and panel. They post updates whenever they want and run their own campaigns. Residents follow them and get notified.

Mobile

Follow official pages with a verified badge, an "Official" tab of updates, notifications for new posts and campaigns, and one-tap join from the page.

Web panel

A community panel to post announcements with photos, create campaigns and missions, see followers and reach. Admins approve each community and set what it may do, for example "campaigns need approval".

2. Volunteer shifts and hours

Effort: medium

Communities and authorities run volunteer days with roles and time slots. Hours should count automatically and be trusted by schools and employers.

Mobile

Sign up for a shift, get reminders, check in and out with a QR at the site, and see hours go straight into the impact passport.

Web panel

Create shifts with roles and capacity, see who signed up, mark attendance, and issue certificates in one click.

3. Authority inbox for citizen reports

Effort: medium

Report & Restore already collects issues. Routing each one to the right authority, with response times, turns GreenifyX into a public service channel.

Mobile

Residents see who is handling their report, progress updates and before/after photos when it's fixed.

Web panel

Each authority gets an inbox for its own area, sets response-time targets, assigns teams and closes reports with a photo. A dashboard shows time to resolve per area.

4. Schools programme

Effort: medium

Schools are a fast route to families. A safe, curriculum-friendly version for classes makes GreenifyX part of the school year.

Mobile

Class accounts with parent consent for under-18s, Eco Academy tracks by grade, and an inter-school league.

Web panel

A teacher dashboard with class progress, assignments from the Academy, a printable class impact certificate and a school-level report.

5. Open data and SDG reporting

Effort: medium to large

Government partners report against national and UN Sustainable Development Goals. Anonymised, aggregated GreenifyX data can feed those reports directly.

Mobile

A public "UAE impact" screen showing totals by emirate and goal, so residents see the bigger picture they're part of.

Web panel

An open-data page and a secure data feed (API) with aggregated, anonymised figures per emirate and month, mapped to SDG targets, plus embeddable charts for partner websites.

Also worth doing soon: face-tracking Eco Lens filters, and Arabic for the remaining server messages.

Before a demo

Do these so nothing fails in front of a partner.

  • Build and install the latest app on two phones and try each new feature once, in English and in Arabic.
  • Wake the server a few minutes early, or move Render to a paid plan so it doesn't sleep.
  • Load demo data with npm run seed:demo, then add a neighbourhood, a mission and a recycling station from the dashboard. Remove demo data afterwards with npm run seed:demo -- --clear.
  • Create a partner login for the partner you're meeting so they can see their own portal.
  • Check the server settings: AI key, Firebase, TURN relay, and the Apple push key for iPhone ringing.
  • Change the TURN relay password, since it was shared in chat.
