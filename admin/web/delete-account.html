Delete your GreenifyX account

GreenifyX is developed by Ramjan Khan. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open GreenifyX and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email

Email ramjankhan8824@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my GreenifyX account". We confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number, password, profile photo and bio
  • Your friends list, friend requests and blocks
  • Notification and call tokens on your devices
  • Utility bills and bill photos
  • Steps and cycling data from Health sync
  • Quick logs, Eco Academy progress and recycling drop-offs
  • Household membership, home neighbourhood and approximate home location
  • Your impact passport and its sharing link
  • Your UAE PASS link (if you used UAE PASS)
  • Open or declined brand-deal applications and your pitch text

What is kept, and why

  • Anonymous impact totals and the points ledger, shown as "Deleted user", so partner reports and leaderboards from the past stay accurate. They are no longer linked to your name, email or phone.
  • Accepted or paid brand-deal records without your pitch, because they are the brand's payment record.
  • Trees you funded in My Forest, without any nickname, because they are real trees planted by partners.
  • Messages you sent stay in your friends' chats, shown from "Deleted user".
  • Short-lived server logs, which our hosting provider deletes automatically after a short period.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Turn off Health sync in the app, and remove GreenifyX's access in Health Connect or the Health app.
  • Delete a typed-in bill in My bills, or email us to delete photo bills.
  • Turn off impact passport sharing in the passport screen.
  • Delete posts and comments from the app, or email us to delete anything else.
