GreenifyX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Applies to the GreenifyX mobile app (Android and iPhone) and the GreenifyX website.

In short: we collect what we need to run your account, verify real environmental actions and reward you. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising. You can delete your account and data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app, or by emailing us.

1. Who we are

GreenifyX is an eco-social app for the United Arab Emirates, developed and operated by Ramjan Khan ("GreenifyX", "we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact us at ramjankhan8824@gmail.com.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: taking photos and videos for posts, scanning QR codes and bills, video calls.
  • Microphone: voice notes, calls and voice commands.
  • Location: verifying presence at sites, recycling stations, issue reports, maps and local missions. Only while you use those features.
  • Photos: choosing pictures to post or send.
  • Notifications and full-screen calls: alerts, and showing an incoming call on the lock screen.
  • Physical activity and Health Connect / Apple Health: reading steps and cycling workouts for Health sync. Optional.
  • Foreground service (microphone and camera): keeping your call going while you use other apps. A notification is shown during the call.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. Only the related feature stops working.

4. Health Connect and Apple Health

With your permission, GreenifyX reads steps, exercise (cycling workouts) and distance for the last 7 days. We send only daily totals to our server to calculate Eco Points. We never write data to Health Connect or Apple Health.

Data from Health Connect and Apple Health is used only to provide Health sync. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used to decide eligibility for credit, insurance or employment. Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can turn Health sync off in the app at any time and revoke access in Health Connect or the Health app.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other members: your name, photo, public posts, badges and leaderboard position. Posts marked "friends only" are seen only by friends.
  • Friends: can verify your posts and message or call you.
  • Organisations and partners you join: see your verified impact in their dashboards and reports (for example actions, volunteer hours and CO₂ estimates). Partners see members' names and totals, not your messages, location history or bills.
  • Field verifiers and moderators: see the evidence you submit and content that is reported.
  • Impact passport: your verified record is visible to anyone with your passport link or QR code only if you switch sharing on.
  • Brands in brand deals: see your application and the post you link to it.

7. Where data is stored

Our providers may store and process data outside the UAE. We choose providers with strong security and contractual protections for international transfers.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Bill photos waiting for confirmation: 30 minutes. Saved bills: until you delete them or your account.
  • UAE PASS sign-in steps: 10 minutes. Cached translations (text only): up to 90 days.
  • Server logs, including call troubleshooting logs: kept only for a short period by our hosting provider.
  • Points ledger and verified impact totals: kept in anonymised form after account deletion, so organisations' historical reports stay accurate.

9. Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. This removes your name, email, phone, photo, bio, friends, push tokens, bills and bill photos, health data, quick logs, lessons, recycling drop-offs, household membership, neighbourhood, impact passport and UAE PASS link, and open brand-deal applications. Your verified impact stays only as anonymous totals. Full details and other ways to ask: Delete your GreenifyX account.

10. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and other laws that may apply to you, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to some uses, withdraw consent at any time, and get a copy of your data. Email ramjankhan8824@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the UAE Data Office.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Access to the admin dashboard is limited to authorised staff, and partner and auditor logins see only what their role allows. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

12. Children

GreenifyX is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
